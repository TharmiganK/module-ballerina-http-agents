**For the defect scanner:**
- `getRedirectMethod` rewrites HEAD to GET on 301/302/303 (RFC 9110 keeps HEAD).
- `getRedirectMethod` follows 300 and 305 automatically (305 is deprecated, and 300 has no single Location semantics).
- A request converted to GET may still carry the original body or `Content-Length`; the sample showed `content-length: 0` sent on the redirected GET.
- `createNewEndpointConfig` passes `auth`, so the auth handler re-adds credentials on cross-origin redirects (related to the closed upstream #8216).
- `performRetryAction` with an `HttpFuture` (`submit`) and a non-matching status returns the original future after the response has already been consumed with `getResponse`.
- In `initialize`, when `circuitBreaker` is set, the redirect and retry wrapping order may differ from the non-CB path.
- Already reported: the extra sleep after the final attempt (#34) and `maxWaitInterval` not capping the first wait (#35).
